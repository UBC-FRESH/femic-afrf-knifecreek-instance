# KC general map (2014)

## Source

`KC_General_Map.pdf` is a 2014 ArcMap general map of the Knife Creek block at
1:30,000. It is a planning/context map, not the Klinka site-series map.

Path: `/srv/shared-data/gep/ws4/tmp/KC_General_Map.pdf` (local; not committed).

## Contents

- UBC research-forest boundary and the Knife Creek block;
- roads, trails, and demonstration/tour routes;
- existing and proposed cutblocks;
- wildlife tree patches;
- pipeline;
- 20 m contours;
- wetlands;
- compartment labels (`BL*`, `BR*`, `JR*`, `WR*`, `WL*`).

## Status

The map was rendered and inspected but not georeferenced or committed. It has no
published coordinate grid in the copy supplied. It is useful for compartment
labels and a visual cross-check, not for area or spatial analysis. The MWP #3
Appendix 9 page-162 map is the better public compartment/habitat source.

A defensible georeference would need control points from the SUP S15382
boundary, roads/lake shoreline, and VRI/cutblock corners, with a residual and
overlay review. That is deferred until the private AFRF GIS or enough control
points are available.
