# Knife Creek sources

This note records the public sources used for context. It is not a substitute
for the data-acquisition manifest.

## AFRF Management and Working Plan #2

- URL: <https://afrf-forestry.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2012/03/MWP-PLN2.pdf>
- Facts used:
  - Knife Creek block area is 3,487 ha.
  - Knife Creek is predominantly IDFdk3, with a small IDFxm component and an
    eastern transition to SBPSmk.
  - Gavin Lake is 6,315 ha and is the other AFRF block.
  - Both blocks are near Williams Lake in the Cariboo Forest Region.

## AFRF block locations

- URL: <https://www.afrf.forestry.ubc.ca/block-locations/>
- Facts used:
  - Knife Creek location reference is UTM zone 10U, NAD83, approximately
    575165 E, 5767955 N.
  - Access is via Big Meadow Road.

## AFRF site units and history

- Site units report, public PDF on the AFRF site.
- History report, public PDF on the AFRF site.
- Facts used:
  - Knife Creek has a dry-belt Douglas-fir character and a mule-deer
    winter-range objective.
  - Harvest history extends back to the 1940s.

## 2013 wildfire report

- URL: <https://afrf-forestry.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2017/10/Daniels-Luu-and-Carroll-Report-Sep-2017.pdf>
- Facts used:
  - A 2013 wildfire burned part of the Knife Creek block.
  - The study area is in IDFdk3 and is dominated by interior Douglas-fir.

## Uncertainty

- The public sources are context. The legal boundary, current inventory, BEC
  mapping, and mule-deer constraints must come from the authoritative layers
  and be recorded in the Phase 1 manifest.
- Do not quote the 3,487 ha figure as the model area without the boundary and
  the net-area calculation.
