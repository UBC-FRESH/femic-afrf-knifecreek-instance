# Knife Creek boundary

The legal Knife Creek block boundary is:

- `knife_creek_sup_S15382.gpkg`
- layer `knife_creek_sup_S15382`
- EPSG:3005

It is the provincial Special Use Permit polygon **S15382** (“Research or
Educational”, ACTIVE), downloaded from the public Forest Tenure Special Use
Permit Polygon layer under the Open Government Licence - British Columbia.

Provenance, area reconciliation, and the exact fetch command are in
`../../metadata/boundary_source.yaml`.

The polygon is the legal gross boundary. The net forested area comes from the
VRI/forest-cover join once that layer is acquired.
