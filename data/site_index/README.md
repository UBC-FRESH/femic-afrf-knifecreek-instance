# Site index

`klinka_2004_site_index.csv` transcribes Tables 8.2.1–8.2.4 of Klinka et al.
2004, *Site Units of the University of British Columbia Alex Fraser Research
Forest* (PDF pages 62–63, printed 53–54).

- 105 rows covering `IDFxm`, `IDFdk3`, `SBSdw1` and `ICHmk3`.
- Species: Douglas-fir, lodgepole pine and hybrid/interior spruce.
- `site_index_m_50` is metres at 50 years breast height.
- `site_index_class` is the report's nominal 5-class scale (1 highest, 5
  lowest).
- `status` records `viable`, `limited`, or `non-viable`.
- `note` keeps limits such as `<9 m` and `19/limited`; they are not silently
  converted to exact values.

The metadata file records the source PDF SHA-256 and the transcription
convention. The report's quantitative content is in these tables; its figures
are photographs and flow diagrams, not quantitative charts.

Do not infer a site series from BEC zone/subzone alone, and do not use these
values as a spatial site-series map. The georeferenced site-series map remains
outstanding under P1.2a.
