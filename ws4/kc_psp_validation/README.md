# AFRF Knife Creek PSP FVS-BC backtest v1

Status: **structural backtest**, not a calibrated KC model.

This version corrects the cycle-year alignment in v0. The earlier run used
four-year FVS cycles and mapped them onto the 2004/2008/2013 measurement years;
it is superseded by the tables below.

## Method

- Initial state: 1993 interior trees (`P = I`) from the 24 AFRF PSP plots.
- Each tree is expanded by 20 (0.05 ha plot).
- FVS-BC runs without cutting, with intervals of 4, 7, 4 and 5 years, so the
  summary years are 1993, 1997, 2004, 2008 and 2013.
- Tree age 100 and site index FD 60 are placeholders.
- Height is filled from DBH when 1993 height is missing.
- Species are mapped to BC codes: FD, SE (SX), PL, EP, AT, OH (W).

## Observed mortality and calibration

Observed annual mortality by interval (interior trees, DBH presence):

| Interval | Annual mortality |
| --- | ---: |
| 1993–1997 | 1.0% |
| 1997–2004 | 1.4% |
| 2004–2008 | 2.1% |
| 2008–2013 | 2.4% |

Recruitment was small and concentrated in thinned plots.

FVS `MORTMULT` sensitivity at 2013:

| Multiplier | Pred BA | BA bias | Pred TPH | TPH bias |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1.0 | 36.42 | +1.04 | 2,713 | +422 |
| 2.0 | 33.38 | -2.00 | 2,417 | +126 |
| 2.5 | 32.13 | -3.25 | 2,290 | -0.5 |
| 3.0 | 31.04 | -4.34 | 2,176 | -115 |
| 4.0 | 28.71 | -6.67 | 1,955 | -336 |

No single global multiplier fits both TPH and BA. Multiplier 2.5 nearly matches
2013 TPH but leaves BA low by 3.25 m²/ha; multiplier 2.0 is a compromise with
BA -2.00 and TPH +126. The remaining BA error needs growth/species calibration,
not just mortality scaling.

A site-index 80 versus 60 test produced no change under this configuration. The
age/site-index treatment therefore remains unresolved. The `MORTMULT` sweep in
`mortality_sensitivity.csv` was generated by repeating the runbook with
`--mortality-multiplier 1`, `2`, `2.5`, `3` and `4`.

## Caveats

- Tree age and site index are placeholders.
- Height fill for missing 1993 heights is crude.
- Species are mapped to BC codes; `W` is mapped to `OH`.
- No cut is applied; this is a growth/mortality test, not a treatment test.
- The 24-plot thinning experiment is not a whole-block sample.
- The raw workbook is private; only derived validation tables are committed.
- v0's 2004/2008/2013 table was misaligned and must not be reused.
