# KC spatial dataset v4 high-regime upper bound

This is a deliberately HMZ-unconstrained copy of `dataset_kc_spatial_v4`. It
offers High, Moderate and Low on every block so the canonical graph can express
its positive high-regime cycles.

## Status

This is an **upper-bound sensitivity**, not a legal-compliance dataset and not
an approved management plan. The legal HMZ matrix is:

- HMZ M: Low or Moderate only;
- HMZ L: Low only;
- HMZ A: all classes (effectively absent inside the SUP).

The HMZ-constrained dataset remains `ws4/dataset_kc_spatial_v4`.

## Result

The high-regime policy sustains production through 300 years. The constrained
policy enters a reserve cycle after about 120 years. The difference is the
model's upper-bound gap, not an approved harvest level.
